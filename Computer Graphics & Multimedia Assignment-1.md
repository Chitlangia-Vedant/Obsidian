# Canva: Features and Hands-on Practice

## Introduction

Canva is an online graphic design platform that allows users to create visual content without requiring advanced design skills. It offers a drag-and-drop interface, ready-made templates, stock photos, icons, fonts, animations, and collaboration tools. Canva is widely used for designing social media posts, presentations, posters, resumes, brochures, certificates, videos, and marketing materials.

The platform is available through a web browser and as a desktop and mobile application. It supports both free and paid plans, making it suitable for students, educators, businesses, and content creators.

---
# Key Features

Canva provides a range of features for graphic design and content creation.

- Drag-and-drop editor for easy design.
- Thousands of ready-made templates.
- Large library of images, icons, illustrations, and shapes.
- Text editing with multiple fonts and styles.
- Background remover and photo editing tools (Pro feature).
- Animation and video editing tools.
- Presentation creation with transitions.
- Brand Kit for consistent branding (Pro feature).
- Real-time collaboration and commenting.
- Export designs in PNG, JPG, PDF, MP4, and GIF formats.

# Representation: Base 32 String

No. of bits: 32

```
_ _ _ _ _ | _ _ _ _ _ | _ _ _ _ _ | _  
    |       
   ...
```

Split 32 bits in 6 '5 bits' and a '2 bit'

For 5 bit -> $2^5$ = 32
	0-25 -> A-Z
	26-31 -> 2-7
	

Example
00000.11010.11001.00101.11001...
0.26.25.5.27
0->A
26->2
25->Z
5->E
27->3
A2ZE3...
